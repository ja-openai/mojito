# Build a small routine

{/* mojito-id: guide.intro */}
A useful routine starts small enough to fit into an ordinary day. Choose a moment you already have, then give it a little purpose.

{/* mojito-id: guide.begin.title */}
## Begin with five minutes

{/* mojito-id: guide.begin.body */}
Read a page over breakfast, take a note after a walk, or talk through an idea with a friend. Consistency matters more than the size of the task.
